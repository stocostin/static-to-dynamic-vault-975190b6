Expert Woodwork Services in Kenya

At Shachavir Timber Limited, we are committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship and personalized solutions for all your woodwork needs. With decades of experience, our team transforms quality timber into stunning doors, frames, staircases, and bespoke furniture. Whether you are building, renovating, or upgrading, we offer reliable service, premium materials, and attention to detail that sets us apart.
