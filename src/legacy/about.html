About Shachavir Timber Limited

Driven by Precision, Delivered with Passion

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Shachavir Timber Limited began as a small workshop with a vision to redefine woodworking in Kenya. What started as a passion project has grown into a respected name in custom woodwork, serving both residential and commercial clients across the country.

Our journey has been guided by an unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the signature of our dedication to the craft.

Our Mission, Vision & Objective

Mission

To deliver exceptional woodwork solutions that enhance the beauty and functionality of spaces, while fostering a culture of craftsmanship and innovation.

Vision

To be the leading provider of bespoke woodwork in Kenya, recognized for our quality, creativity, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Objective

To be a trusted leader in premium timber solutions by delivering quality craftsmanship, exceeding client expectations, and promoting sustainable, innovative joinery.

Our Process

At Shachavir Timber Limited, we follow a structured process that ensures every project meets our high standards and your expectations:

Consultation

We begin with a one-on-one consultation to understand your needs, preferences, and budget.

Design & Planning

Our team creates tailored designs and recommends the best timber solutions for your project.

Material Selection

We help you choose high-quality, durable timber sourced responsibly.

Craftsmanship

Our skilled artisans bring the design to life with precision and care.

Quality Checks

Every piece undergoes thorough inspection to ensure excellence.

Delivery & Installation

We deliver and, if required, install the final product with minimal disruption.

From concept to completion, we prioritize clear communication, timely delivery, and customer satisfaction.
